About PURCELALEX

Independent web development, QA testing, and web writing built on more than two decades of hands-on sales, marketing, and business development experience — including 17 years with Philip Morris.

My name is Alexandru Purcel, and I'm based in Râșcani, Republic of Moldova. PURCELALEX is built on more than two decades of hands-on sales, marketing, and business development experience — including 17 years with Philip Morris, where I grew from Specialist in Retail Trade Development to Senior Territory Executive to Business Builder, driving sales growth for major brands including Marlboro, Parliament, Chesterfield, L&M, Bond Street, and IQOS.

Before that, my career spanned construction and building-systems sales, FMCG distribution with Coca-Cola Bottling Chișinău, and six years as an elected member of the Râșcani City Council — giving me a practical, ground-level understanding of sales, negotiation, and how businesses actually grow.

My story

After 26 years in sales, I decided to change my life. Technology had interested me for a long time, and I'm self-driven when it comes to learning it — I started with Harvard's CS50 course with David Malan, then kept going through W3Schools, Udemy, and countless other courses and resources online. AI tools have been part of that learning process too.

One month ago, after a vacation with my family, I left my permanent job. I'm now working as a freelancer — building websites, testing them thoroughly, and writing the copy that goes on them — with the same discipline that carried me through two decades in sales.

My approach

My approach comes from the field, not a classroom. Two decades spent building sales territories, growing brand portfolios, and developing retail trade relationships taught me what actually moves the needle in a business — and what's just noise.

I bring that same practical, results-first mindset to web development, QA testing, and web writing: clear priorities, honest communication, and work built for how your project actually needs to run, not a generic template.
