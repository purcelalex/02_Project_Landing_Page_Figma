About PURCELALEX

Independent consulting built on more than two decades of hands-on sales, marketing, and business development experience — including 17 years with Philip Morris.

PURCELALEX is built on more than two decades of hands-on sales, marketing, and business development experience — including 17 years with Philip Morris, where I grew from Specialist in Retail Trade Development to Senior Territory Executive to Business Builder, driving sales growth for major brands including Marlboro, Parliament, Chesterfield, L&M, Bond Street, and IQOS.

Before that, my career spanned construction and building-systems sales, FMCG distribution with Coca-Cola Bottling Chișinău, and six years as an elected member of the Râșcani City Council — giving me a practical, ground-level understanding of sales, negotiation, and how businesses actually grow.

My approach

My approach comes from the field, not a classroom. Two decades spent building sales territories, growing brand portfolios, and developing retail trade relationships taught me what actually moves the needle in a business — and what's just noise.

I bring that same practical, results-first mindset to consulting, coaching, and analytics: clear priorities, honest feedback, and recommendations built for how your business actually operates, not a generic framework.
