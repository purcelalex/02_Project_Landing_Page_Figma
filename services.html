How engagements work

Every engagement starts with a conversation. We talk through where your business stands today, what isn't working, and what a good outcome would actually look like — no templates, no assumptions about your industry before I understand it.

From there I put together a scoped proposal: what I'll deliver, the timeline, and the price. You approve it before any work begins, so there are no surprises partway through.

What to expect

Expect direct, practical recommendations you can act on immediately — not a long report that sits unread. Every engagement ends with a clear set of next steps, not just a summary of the problem.

For ongoing coaching or analytics work, we set a regular check-in cadence so progress stays on track and priorities can shift as your business does.
