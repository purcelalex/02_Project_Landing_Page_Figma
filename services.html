How we will collaborate

Every project starts with you sharing detailed requirements — what app needs to do, who it's for, and any technical constraints I should plan around.

From there, I design the technical architecture: how the pages, components, and data fit together, and how it maps to your design. I submit that architecture to you for approval before any development starts, so we're aligned on the approach up front. Once you approve it, I build in scoped stages, test as I go, and keep you updated at each milestone through to delivery.

What to expect

Expect clean code, clear bug reports, and accurate, usable data — not a black box you have to take on faith. Every engagement ends with working, tested deliverables.

For ongoing QA testing or data cleaning work, we set a regular check-in cadence so progress stays on track and priorities can shift as your project does. New to working together? Send a request and I'll do a free test task first, so you can see how I work before committing to anything bigger.
